Pint's Book Hunt v1.0 - Friend of the Library checklist
(WoW Forever; loads on Classic Era too)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. With Pint's Quest Book its arrow points the way to each book (otherwise TomTom, or the game's map pin).

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\PintsBookHunt
  Restart the game.

WHAT IT DOES
  - All 40 books for Friend of the Library: name, zone, location and notes (solo, group, hard).
  - Ticks a book off by itself when you read it, loot it or have it in your bags - or tick it yourself.
  - Books count per character (you can collect them again on another character for its rewards).
  - Way: points an arrow at the book. Video: a link to a guide for that book (copy it into your browser).
    "All 40" links one video that collects every book by the shortest route.
  - Book icons on the world map for the books you still need. Ctrl-click one for the way there.
  - Rewards:
      10 books - Friend of the Library: choose Scholarly Pendant or Erudite's Amulet
      20 books - Greater Friend of the Library: choose Field Researcher's Loop or Philanthropist's Ring
    Hand them in to Garion Wendell, top of the Mage Tower in Stormwind (Alliance),
    or Owen Thadd in Undercity (Horde).

HOW TO USE
  /books           the checklist
  /books next      point the way to a book you still need (in your zone first)
  /books reset     untick every book for this character
