Pint's Book Hunt - Changelog

v1.0.1
- Rewards are the real items: the game's tooltip, Shift-click to link them in chat, Ctrl-click to try them on.
- The hand-in button points the way to your librarian (Garion Wendell in Stormwind's Mage Quarter, Owen Thadd in
  Undercity's Magic Quarter).
- Minimap button: click for the checklist, right-click for the way to a book you still need; your count in its
  tooltip. Drag it round the minimap; /books minimap hides it.
- Rumi of Gnomeregan: The Collected Works shows both places it's found (Thelsamar inn, Loch Modan, and the Sentinel
  Hill inn, Westfall).
- WoW Forever: fixed errors opening the checklist and hovering the rewards.

v1.0
- First release: a checklist of the 40 books for Friend of the Library (WoW Forever) - book, zone, location and
  how hard it is (solo, group, hard), with a guide video for each and one for all 40 by the shortest route.
- Books tick themselves off when you read, loot or carry them (or tick them by hand). Counted per character, with
  how many your other characters have.
- "Way" points Pint's Quest Book's arrow at a book (or TomTom's, or the game's map pin).
- Book icons on the world map for the books you still need (Ctrl-click one for the way there).
- The rewards at 10 and 20 books, who to hand them to for your faction, and whether you're ready or done.
